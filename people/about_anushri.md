I am an Assistant Professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering](https://www.mae.ucla.edu). Prior to this, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at [Princeton University](https://mae.princeton.edu) in the [Intelligent Robot Motion group](https://irom-lab.princeton.edu). I earned my Ph.D. in [Control and Dynamical Systems from California Institute of Technology](https://www.cms.caltech.edu) in 2023 and my B.S. in [Electrical Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology](https://ece.gatech.edu) in 2017. 

My research focuses on motion planning and control of robots in unstructured environments while accounting for uncertainty in a principled manner.  I have received the Outstanding Student Paper Award at the Conference on Decision and Control, Best Student Paper Award at the Conference of Robot Learning (as a co-author), and was selected as a Rising Star in Data Science by The University of Chicago.
