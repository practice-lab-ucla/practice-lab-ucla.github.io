I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Aerospace Engineering in the Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (<strong>MAE</strong>) department at the University of California, Los Angeles (<strong>UCLA</strong>).

I am currently a graduate researcher at the UCLA PRACTICE Lab, advised by Professor Anushri Dixit. Before UCLA, I worked as a Guidance, Navigation, and Control scientist at the [Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)](https://www.isro.gov.in/), developing autonomy and control algorithms for various interplanetary and geostationary spacecraft missions, including Chandrayaan-3 Moon landing. At UCLA, my research focuses on safe and reliable autonomous systems spanning reinforcement learning, motion planning, optimal control, and multi-agent decision-making.
